Fill in the check on the left, then Preview check — the PDF opens in its own window, so this form stays where it is.

Print from that window at 100% scale / “Actual size” (no fit-to-page) onto your pre-printed check-on-top stock. Then come back here and click “Printed correctly — record it” — nothing is recorded until you do. If a sheet jams, bump the Check # and preview again.
